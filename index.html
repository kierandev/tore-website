What's new

Recent changes in development, and what's coming next.

20 Nov

Built-in bug reporter. Press ⇧⌘B, mark the problem on a screenshot, choose the part of TORE and add a note.

20 Nov

Field theme. A black aluminium finish. Only materials change – every control stays in place.

20 Nov

Sampler and audio fixes. Loops stop at the sample's ends, and Bluetooth headphones recover when another app takes the mic.

20 Nov

Cleaner resizing. Every unit fits its contents at any size and zoom.

Next

Horizontal zoom in the piano roll, a tempo-synced LFO and more modulation for ONE, a silver Field finish, and an FX send button on the Canvas mixer.

FAQ

Can I buy TORE?

Not yet. TORE is still in pre-alpha and isn't ready for a public release. Email support@toreaudio.com and we'll tell you when it's available.

What does pre-alpha mean?

TORE works, but it isn't finished. Features are still being added and changed, and it's being tested and fixed every day.

What will it run on?

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later).

How do I get in touch?

Email support@toreaudio.com.
