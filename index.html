Features

Add the units you need to a canvas, move them like windows and connect them together. Every unit stays readable at any zoom.

Decks

Neon waveforms on a beat grid, SYNC and MASTER, eight colour-coded hot cues, loops, quantise and pitch with a selectable range. Title, artist, time left, BPM and key always stay in view.

Sampler

Drop a sample on a deck to turn it into a sampler, or drop a track back on to make it a deck again. Loop and trim directly on the strip.

Mixer

A channel strip for every deck and sampler, with High, Mid, Low and Filter, auto gain, headphone cue and send/return effects.

Drum machine

Pad-based patterns with support for your own kits and sample folders.

ONE FM synth

A built-in FM synthesiser with a visual panel for shaping and playing sounds.

Keys and sequencer

Play instruments from a MIDI keyboard and write parts in the piano roll, with snap, nudge and swing.

Music library

Multiple library support. Browse and search your collection and load tracks straight to any deck or sampler.

Effects

Channel inserts and send/return effects shared across decks.

Timeline

Record a set or a session, then refine it with automation.

MIDI controllers

Link a controller and map it to the units you use.

Themes

Switch the look of the interface without anything moving. Play, CUE and SYNC keep their colours in every theme.

Three views

Switch between them from the top bar. The same units appear in each view, so nothing needs setting up twice.

Canvas

Lay out every unit, patch keys and MIDI, design sounds and build patterns.

Perform

Two or four decks around the mixer and library, laid out for playing live.

Timeline

Capture what you play and shape it afterwards with automation.

FAQ

What does early alpha mean?

TORE is in active development. Builds arrive often, features will change, and you may run into bugs – report them with the built-in bug reporter.

What are the requirements?

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later).

Is it a subscription?

No. It's a single £20 payment that covers every future version.

Can I use it on a new Mac?

Yes. Download TORE again and enter your serial. If you've lost it, email support@toreaudio.com.

How do I get support?

Use the bug reporter inside TORE, or email support@toreaudio.com.
