Features

Your studio and your booth, together.

Add the units you need to a canvas, move them like windows and link them together. Every unit stays readable at any zoom.

Decks
SYNCMASTER124.008A

Mix with everything in view

Full-colour waveforms on a beat grid, SYNC and MASTER, eight colour-coded hot cues, loops, quantise and pitch with a selectable range. Title, artist, time left, BPM and key stay visible at every size.

Mixer
AUTO GAIN

A strip for every deck

High, Mid, Low and Filter, faders that catch at 0 dB, auto gain, headphone cue and send/return effects on every deck and sampler.

Production

Drums, samples and synths

A drum machine with your own kits, a sampler that swaps with any deck, the ONE FM synth, and a sequencer with a piano roll, snap, nudge and swing.

Timeline
RecordSNAPREAD● WRITE

Record it, then refine it

Hit Record and every mixer channel and the master land on their own lanes, with your fader, EQ and filter moves. Then trim, split and shape it with automation.

Music libraryMultiple library support. Search your collection, see matching keys and load to any deck or sampler.
EffectsChannel inserts and send/return effects shared across decks.
MIDI controllersLink a controller and map it to the units you use.
KeysPlay any instrument from a MIDI keyboard.
ThemesChange the finish of the interface. Play, CUE and SYNC keep their colours in every theme.

Views

Three views. One setup.

Switch from the top bar. The same units appear in every view, so nothing needs setting up twice.

Canvas

Lay out every unit, link keys and MIDI, design sounds and build patterns.

Perform

Two or four decks around the mixer and library, laid out for playing live.

Timeline

Capture what you play and shape it afterwards with automation.

What's new

Built in the open.

Recent changes in development, and what's coming next.

21 Nov

Smoother playback. Zoomed waveforms now move on the GPU, so four decks and the drum machine stay smooth.

21 Nov

MASTER hand-over. When the MASTER deck stops, another playing deck takes over without the tempo jumping. Mixer faders now catch at 0 dB with a dB scale.

20 Nov

Built-in bug reporter. Press ⇧⌘B, mark the problem on a screenshot, choose the part of TORE and add a note.

20 Nov

Field theme. A black aluminium finish. Only materials change – every control stays in place.

Next

Serum-style curve editing for ONE's envelopes and LFOs, horizontal zoom in the piano roll, and a silver Field finish.

FAQ

Questions

Can I buy TORE?

Not yet. TORE is still in pre-alpha and isn't ready for a public release. Email support@toreaudio.com and we'll tell you when it's available.

What does pre-alpha mean?

TORE works, but it isn't finished. Features are still being added and changed, and it's being tested and fixed every day.

What will it run on?

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later).

How do I get in touch?
